Dick Hardy is Co-Founder of The Hardy Group, a Pastoral Leadership Consulting non-profit for lead pastors. He has served as an Administrative Pastor, Chief Operating Officer, Non-Profit Executive Director and College Vice-President, and consults and coaches personally with pastors who are tired of the status quo and want to see substantive change and growth in the ministries they serve.
