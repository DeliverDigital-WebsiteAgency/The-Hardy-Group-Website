Dick Hardy is co-founder of The Hardy Group and Leaders.Church. After nearly 30 years in church ministry as an administrative pastor, church business administrator, COO, and college vice president, he founded The Hardy Group in 2007 and has since coached several hundred pastors one-on-one. With his son, Jonathan, he launched Leaders.Church to help pastors grow their leadership and their churches. Dick and his wife, Pat, have two children and seven grandchildren.
