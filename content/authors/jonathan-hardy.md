Jonathan Hardy is Co-Founder of The Hardy Group, a Pastoral Leadership Consulting non-profit for lead pastors. He has served as a large church staff pastor, church planter, author and speaker, and consults and coaches personally with pastors who are tired of the status quo and want to see substantive change and growth in the ministries they serve.
