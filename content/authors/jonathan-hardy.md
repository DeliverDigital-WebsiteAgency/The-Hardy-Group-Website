Jonathan Hardy is co-founder of Leaders.Church. He served on the executive leadership team at James River Church in Springfield, Missouri, then helped plant Summit Park Church in Kansas City, where he oversaw finances and operations as the church grew to nearly 1,900 in weekend attendance. He is the author of Arrow Striker: Live with Purpose and Leave an Eternal Impact… Every Single Day. Jonathan and his wife, Ashley, have three children.
